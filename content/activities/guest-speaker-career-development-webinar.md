## Building Professional Identity: Career Development Webinar

Served as a guest speaker at a career development webinar organized by Government Postgraduate College for Women, Mandian, Abbottabad.

The two-part online workshop was conducted on August 29–30, 2026.

## Topics Covered

- CV and resume development
- LinkedIn profile optimization
- Building a professional identity
- Presenting skills and experience effectively
- Career development for students

The session focused on helping students understand how to communicate their academic background, skills, and professional direction through their CVs and LinkedIn profiles.
