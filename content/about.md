## How I Approach Problems

I am interested in technology because of what it can solve.

My approach begins with understanding a real problem, identifying the bottleneck, and determining where software, AI, or automation can make a practical difference.

I prefer building systems that address actual requirements rather than creating demonstrations around technologies without a clear purpose.

My general process is:

1. Identify a real problem.
2. Understand the existing workflow and its limitations.
3. Design an appropriate technical solution.
4. Build and test the solution.
5. Deploy and document the work.
6. Evaluate its usefulness and identify improvements.

I am particularly interested in reducing repetitive work, improving access to information, automating inefficient workflows, and developing software that can be used beyond a demonstration.
